# India telecom and privacy readiness (October 2026)

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Obtain legal and operator review before placing any real recruitment calls.

TRAI regulates unsolicited commercial communication, including voice calling, under TCCCPR 2018 and subsequent instructions. Do not assume all recruitment calls are exempt. Check applicable sender registration, DND/consumer preferences, consent and carrier-approved caller IDs/routes.
Official references:
https://www.trai.gov.in/advice-to-senders
https://www.trai.gov.in/what-spam-or-ucc
https://trai.gov.in/faqcategory/unsolicited-commercial-communicationsucc

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 follow phased commencement. Gazette G.S.R. 843(E), 13 November 2025:
https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf
MeitY official legislation:
https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies

Before real calls: independently verify candidate authorization, provide clear purpose/notice, maintain opt-out suppression, retention/deletion processes, prevent sensitive data exposure, protect call records, restrict system operators, audit access, honor contact preferences and implement DND/commercial-calling obligations as applicable. Do not treat an uploaded consent=yes cell as sufficient proof of legal consent. Do not use a personal mobile SIM for unauthorized promotional calling.

Current MVP: local consent flag/time/source, audit records, global opt-out and disabled public dialling. Missing for production: verifiable consent evidence, policy-based calling windows, secure data deletion workflows, multi-user audit access controls, carrier registration/DLT integration where applicable, and organizational privacy governance.
